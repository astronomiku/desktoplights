Quick answers

Where does Desktop Lights run?

It runs as a macOS menu bar app. There is no regular Dock icon, and the lights sit around your selected screen edges.

Can I use more than one display?

Yes. Desktop Lights supports multi-display setups and keeps the decoration out of your working content.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use Restore Purchases inside Desktop Lights. The Mac App Store securely restores eligible purchases associated with your Apple Account.

Do the lights work offline?

Yes. Installed collections and your saved settings remain available offline.

How do I turn the lights off quickly?

Use the menu bar control or your configured keyboard shortcut.

Does the app record my screen?

No. Desktop Lights does not capture your screen, read your documents, or track your activity.
