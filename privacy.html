Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Desktop Lights is designed to decorate your Mac without watching what you do on it. The app does not capture your screen, read your documents, track your activity, or sell personal information.

Information stored on your Mac

The app stores your selected collection, light style, colors, animation, placement, keyboard shortcut, launch preference, and purchase-entitlement state locally on your Mac. This information is used only to remember your settings and provide features you selected.

App Store purchases

Purchases made in the Mac App Store are processed by Apple. Desktop Lights receives the product identifier and transaction status needed to unlock or restore a purchase. We do not receive or store your Apple ID, payment-card details, or billing information. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Direct-download licenses

If you use a direct-download edition and choose to activate a paid collection, the app sends the following information to the Desktop Lights license service:

  • The email address used for the purchase
  • The collection unlock code
  • A randomly generated installation identifier
  • The platform name, macOS

This information is used to verify the purchase, deliver the correct entitlements, prevent unauthorized sharing, and enforce the permitted device limit. The installation identifier is generated by the app and is not a hardware serial number.

Collection updates and network information

The direct-download edition may contact the Desktop Lights service to check for collection metadata and updates. As with any internet request, service and hosting providers may process standard technical information such as an IP address, request time, browser or app information, and basic server logs for security and reliable operation.

Website

The Desktop Lights website does not provide user accounts or advertising tracking. It may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can cause standard technical request information such as your IP address and browser details to be processed by Google under Google's privacy terms. If analytics, mailing-list forms, or additional services are added later, this policy will be updated before those services are used.

Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers when necessary to operate purchases, licensing, hosting, updates, or support; when you ask us to do so; or when required by law. App Store transactions are handled by Apple, and direct purchases may be handled by the purchase provider shown at checkout.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences remain on your Mac until you reset them or remove the app and its stored data. License records are retained only as long as reasonably needed to provide purchased access, prevent abuse, resolve support issues, and meet legal or accounting obligations. You may contact us to request access to or deletion of personal information we control, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

Children's privacy

Desktop Lights is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as Desktop Lights evolves. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
