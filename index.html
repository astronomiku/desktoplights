How it works

Four steps, all from the menu bar. No dock icon, no clutter — just lights.

01

Pick a collection

Choose from themed sets of hand-designed bulbs — everything from classic glass and Christmas icons to Japanese origami and Filipino parols.

02

Choose a preset

Every collection ships with multiple bulb designs and color presets, so you can match your mood or the season in a click.

03

Add a garland or greeting

Select collections layer on garlands, wreaths, or holiday greeting text strung right alongside the bulbs.

04

Let them glow

Lights drape along your screen edges and twinkle in the background while you work — toggle them on or off anytime from the menu bar.
